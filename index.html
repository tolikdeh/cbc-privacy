CbC Analytics: Privacy Policy

CbC Analytics is a private internal tool of the YouTube channel Concept by Concept. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

Once a day it reads the statistics of our own YouTube channel (such as views, watch time, likes and traffic sources) through the official YouTube Analytics API and copies them into our own private Google Sheet.

What data it uses

  • Only aggregated statistics of our own channel and our own videos, with read-only access.
  • It does not read, collect or store any personal data about viewers, and it does not access anyone else's account.
  • The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Access and removal

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which stops the tool.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
